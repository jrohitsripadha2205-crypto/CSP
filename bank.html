Security Best Practices

Keep your financial information secure with these tips:

  • Create strong, unique passwords for banking sites
  • Recognize and avoid phishing scams and fraudulent emails
  • Enable two-factor authentication for added security
  • Secure your home Wi-Fi network with a strong password
  • Monitor accounts regularly for suspicious activity
  • Never share banking credentials with anyone
  • Use secure connections (look for HTTPS in the URL)

Digital Payments

Getting Started with Online Banking

1

Contact Your Bank

Call your bank's customer service to enroll in online banking. They will guide you through the process and provide necessary credentials. Have your account information ready.

2

Create a Secure Password

Choose a strong password that includes uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Avoid using easily guessable information like birthdays or names.

3

Explore the Interface

Once logged in, take time to explore the different sections. Start with checking your account balance and recent transactions to familiarize yourself with the layout.

4

Set Up Alerts

Configure account alerts to receive notifications for large transactions, low balances, or suspicious activity. This helps you monitor your accounts easily.

5

Practice with Small Transactions

Start with small transfers or bill payments to build confidence before handling larger transactions. You can always cancel most transactions within a short window.

6

Explore Additional Features

Once comfortable with basic functions, explore additional features like mobile deposit, spending reports, and budgeting tools that your bank may offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online banking safe? +

When proper security measures are followed, online banking is very safe. Banks use encryption and other security technologies to protect your information. We'll teach you how to recognize secure websites (look for "https://" and a padlock icon in the address bar) and avoid common scams. Additionally, most banks offer fraud protection guarantees for online banking.

What if I make a mistake in a transaction? +

Most banking transactions can be reviewed before finalizing, and many can be canceled if done promptly. For example, bill payments can often be canceled up to a certain time before the processing date. Transfers between your own accounts can typically be reversed within a short window. We'll show you how to double-check transactions and where to find help if you encounter problems.

Can I still visit my local branch if I use online banking? +

Yes, online banking doesn't eliminate traditional banking methods. You can still visit branches, use ATMs, and speak with tellers while enjoying the convenience of online services for other transactions. Many people use a combination of both based on their needs and comfort level.

What should I do if I suspect fraudulent activity? +

Contact your bank immediately if you notice any suspicious activity. Most banks have 24/7 fraud hotlines. We can help you recognize signs of fraud and take appropriate action. It's also important to monitor your accounts regularly and set up alerts for unusual activity.

Are there fees for using online banking? +

Most banks do not charge fees for basic online banking services like viewing accounts, transferring money between your accounts, or paying bills. Some may charge for expedited payments or wire transfers. We can help you understand your bank's specific fee structure.
