Preparing for Your First Telehealth Visit

1

Gather Your Information

Collect your insurance card, list of medications, pharmacy information, and any recent medical records.

2

Test Your Technology

Check your internet connection, camera, microphone, and speakers. Download any necessary apps in advance.

3

Find a Quiet, Private Space

Choose a well-lit area where you won't be interrupted. Have a notepad and pen ready for taking notes.

4

Prepare Your Questions

Write down your symptoms, questions, and concerns beforehand to make the most of your appointment time.

5

Join the Session Early

Log in 10-15 minutes before your appointment to address any technical issues and complete pre-visit forms.

6

Follow Up Afterward

Review next steps, schedule follow-up appointments if needed, and access your visit summary through the patient portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is telehealth as effective as in-person visits? +

For many routine appointments, follow-ups, and certain consultations, telehealth can be just as effective as in-person visits. However, some conditions still require physical examination. Your doctor will determine if telehealth is appropriate for your needs.

Does Medicare cover telehealth services? +

Yes, Medicare Part B covers telehealth services. During the COVID-19 pandemic, coverage was expanded, and many of these expanded benefits have been made permanent. You'll typically pay the same amount as you would for an in-person visit.

What equipment do I need for a telehealth visit? +

You'll need a smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone. A reliable internet connection is also important. Some visits can be conducted by phone if video isn't available. We can help you test your equipment before your appointment.

Can prescriptions be prescribed through telehealth? +

Yes, doctors can prescribe medications during telehealth visits, with some exceptions for controlled substances that may require an in-person visit depending on state regulations. Prescriptions are sent electronically to your pharmacy.
