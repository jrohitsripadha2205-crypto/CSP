Setting Up Video Calls

1

Choose a Video Call App

Popular options include FaceTime (for iPhone users), WhatsApp, Zoom, or Facebook Messenger. We can help you decide which is best for your needs.

2

Download and Install the App

Go to the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android), search for the app, and tap "Install" or "Get".

3

Create an Account if Needed

Some apps require creating an account with your email address and phone number. We can help with this process.

4

Learn the Basic Controls

Practice making test calls to learn how to start/end calls, mute your microphone, and turn your video on/off.

5

Add Contacts

Add family and friends to your app so you can easily connect with them for video calls.

6

Make Your First Call

Select a contact and start your first video call! Remember to hold your phone horizontally for a better view.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make the text larger on my phone? +

You can increase text size in your phone's settings. On iPhone: Go to Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size. On Android: Go to Settings > Display > Font Size. We can help you adjust these settings to make your phone easier to read.

What's the difference between Wi-Fi and cellular data? +

Wi-Fi connects to the internet through a router in your home or elsewhere, usually with no additional cost. Cellular data uses your phone plan's data allowance and works anywhere with cell service. We recommend using Wi-Fi when possible to save your cellular data.

How do I know if my phone is charged? +

When your phone is charging, you'll see a battery icon on the screen, often with a lightning bolt symbol. The battery icon will fill up as it charges. Most phones show a percentage number that indicates exactly how much charge remains.

What should I do if my phone gets wet? +

First, turn it off immediately if it's on. Don't charge it. Dry it with a soft cloth and place it in a bowl of uncooked rice or silica gel packets for 24-48 hours. If it still doesn't work, take it to a repair shop. Better yet, we can help you get a waterproof case to prevent this issue.
